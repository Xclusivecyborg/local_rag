# Stridewell Footwear — Company Overview

Stridewell Footwear Limited is a Nigerian footwear retailer founded in March 2019
by Adaeze Nwosu and Tunde Balogun. The company began as a single stall in Balogun
Market, Lagos Island, and now operates four retail locations and one online store.

## Registration and structure

Stridewell Footwear Limited is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission
under RC 1544820. The registered office is 14B Adeola Odeku Street, Victoria
Island, Lagos. The company is a private limited company with two directors,
Adaeze Nwosu (Managing Director) and Tunde Balogun (Operations Director).

## Headcount

As of 1 July 2026 the company employs 23 people in total. The breakdown is:

- Retail floor staff: 12
- Warehouse and logistics: 4
- Customer support: 3
- Finance and admin: 2
- Leadership: 2

Of the 23, 19 are full-time employees and 4 are on part-time contracts. The
part-time roles are all on the retail floor and cover weekend shifts.

## Locations

Stridewell operates the following locations:

1. **Victoria Island flagship** — 14B Adeola Odeku Street, Lagos. Opened
   September 2021. Largest store by floor area and by revenue.
2. **Ikeja City Mall concession** — Alausa, Ikeja, Lagos. Opened June 2022.
3. **Wuse 2 branch** — 8 Aminu Kano Crescent, Abuja. Opened February 2024.
   This is the only location outside Lagos.
4. **Port Harcourt kiosk** — Genesis Centre, GRA Phase 2. Opened November 2025
   as a six-month pilot, since extended to December 2026.

The online store runs on a hosted platform and ships nationwide via two
third-party couriers.

## Product lines

The catalogue is organised into four lines:

- **Everyday** — canvas and casual sneakers, the volume line, priced ₦18,000
  to ₦35,000.
- **Formal** — leather oxfords, derbies and loafers, priced ₦45,000 to ₦120,000.
- **Athletic** — running and training shoes, priced ₦30,000 to ₦85,000.
- **Heritage** — hand-finished leather goods produced in Aba, priced ₦95,000
  to ₦240,000. This is the highest-margin line and the smallest by volume.

## Trading hours

Retail locations open 9:00 to 19:00 Monday through Saturday, and 12:00 to 18:00
on Sundays. The Port Harcourt kiosk closes at 17:00 on Sundays. All locations
close on public holidays except Boxing Day.
