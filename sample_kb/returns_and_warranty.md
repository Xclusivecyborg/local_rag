# Returns, Exchanges and Warranty Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. This policy supersedes the version dated March 2024.

## Returns window

Customers may return unworn footwear within **21 days** of purchase for a full
refund, provided the original receipt or order number is presented and the shoes
are returned in their original box.

Items bought during a promotional sale may be exchanged within 21 days but are
**not eligible for cash refunds**. This includes Black Friday, Easter and
end-of-season clearance purchases.

The Heritage line carries an extended **45-day** returns window, reflecting its
higher price point and made-to-order finishing.

## Condition requirements

Footwear is accepted for return only if:

- The soles show no outdoor wear. Trying shoes on indoors is fine.
- The original box is intact, including tissue and any dust bag.
- All tags remain attached.

Shoes that have been worn outdoors are assessed case by case by the store
manager. A partial refund of up to 60% may be offered at the manager's
discretion where the item is resaleable as ex-display stock.

## Manufacturing warranty

All footwear carries a **six-month manufacturing warranty** from the date of
purchase, covering sole separation, stitching failure and hardware defects such
as broken eyelets or buckles.

The Heritage line carries a **24-month** warranty on the same terms, plus one
free resole within the first two years.

The warranty does not cover:

- Normal wear to soles, heels or insoles
- Water damage to suede or untreated leather
- Damage from third-party repairs or aftermarket modifications
- Discolouration from prolonged direct sunlight

## Online orders

Online customers have the same 21-day window, counted from the delivery date
rather than the order date. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the
item was faulty or incorrectly shipped, in which case Stridewell covers it and
issues a ₦3,500 courier credit.

Refunds are processed within 7 working days of the returned item arriving at the
Victoria Island warehouse.

## Escalation

Disputes that cannot be resolved by the store manager are escalated to the
Operations Director. Customers may also contact support on 0700-STRIDE or
support@stridewell.example. Support aims to respond within one working day.
