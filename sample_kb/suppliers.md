# Supplier Directory and Terms

Last reviewed 12 May 2026 by Tunde Balogun.

## Aba Leatherworks Cooperative

Supplies all Heritage line footwear. Based in Ariaria International Market, Aba,
Abia State. Primary contact is Mr. Emeka Okafor.

- Payment terms: 50% deposit on order, balance on delivery
- Lead time: 6 to 8 weeks for a standard run of 40 pairs
- Minimum order: 20 pairs per style
- Quality note: hand-finished, so colour varies slightly between production runs.
  Customers are told this in-store; it is a feature of the line, not a defect.

Aba Leatherworks is the sole supplier for Heritage. This is a known
single-supplier risk and the board agreed in the March 2026 review to identify a
second workshop before the end of the year. No second supplier has been onboarded
as of this review.

## Vantage Imports Nigeria Ltd

Supplies the Everyday and Athletic lines, sourced from manufacturers in Vietnam
and China and cleared through Apapa port.

- Payment terms: net 30 from invoice date
- Lead time: 10 to 14 weeks door to door, longer in Q4
- Minimum order: ₦4,000,000 per container consignment
- Reliability: two shipments were delayed beyond 16 weeks in 2025, both due to
  port clearance rather than the manufacturer.

## Corso Formale SRL

Supplies the Formal line. Italian manufacturer, imported via a Lagos-based agent.

- Payment terms: letter of credit, 60 days
- Lead time: 12 weeks
- Minimum order: 60 pairs mixed sizes
- Quality note: consistently the lowest defect rate of any supplier, under 1%.

## Packaging and consumables

Boxes, tissue, dust bags and shoe trees come from Lagos Packaging Solutions on
net 14 terms with a two-week lead time. This is a low-risk, easily substituted
supplier and there are three alternatives quoted in the procurement file.

## Currency exposure

Vantage and Corso Formale both invoice in foreign currency, so roughly 62% of
cost of goods is exposed to naira exchange rate movement. Aba Leatherworks
invoices in naira, which is one reason the Heritage line holds margin better
during currency swings.
