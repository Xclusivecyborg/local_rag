# Staff Handbook (Extract)

Version 4.1, issued February 2026.

## Working hours

Full-time retail staff work a 40-hour week across five days, rostered by the
store manager two weeks in advance. Warehouse staff start at 07:00 to receive
deliveries before stores open.

Anyone rostered on a Sunday receives a shift premium of ₦4,000 on top of normal
pay. Public holiday shifts are paid at 1.5x.

## Leave

Full-time employees accrue **20 working days** of paid annual leave per year,
rising to 25 days after three years of continuous service. Part-time staff
accrue pro rata.

Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks ahead through the store
manager. No more than two floor staff per location may be on leave at the same
time. Leave is not normally approved during December.

Sick leave is paid for up to 10 working days per year. A medical note is required
from the third consecutive day.

Maternity leave is 16 weeks fully paid. Paternity leave is 2 weeks fully paid.

## Staff discount

All employees receive a **40% discount** on Everyday, Formal and Athletic lines,
and **20%** on the Heritage line. The discount extends to immediate family, up to
four pairs per employee per calendar year. Discounted purchases cannot be resold
and are not eligible for cash refunds.

## Commission

Retail floor staff earn commission of 2% on personal sales above a monthly
threshold of ₦800,000. Commission on Heritage line sales is 4%, reflecting the
longer sales conversation those items require. Commission is paid with the
following month's salary.

## Probation and reviews

New employees serve a three-month probation. Performance reviews happen twice a
year, in January and July. Salary adjustments take effect from 1 April.

## Conduct

Staff must not discuss customer purchase history outside the business. Customer
contact details in the till system are for order fulfilment and warranty claims
only, and may not be used for personal outreach.

Handling of customer data follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act. Any suspected
data breach must be reported to the Operations Director the same day.
